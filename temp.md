Subject: EL CID week at SSR-Leiden

Dear local resident

From August 11th to 15th, Leiden University and Leiden University of Applied Sciences' annual introduction week, EL CID week, will take place. More than 1,000 students from Leiden University and University of Applied Sciences will come to Leiden to get to know the city, their studies, and, of course, the student associations. SSR will open its doors to these new students during the week.

While this will undoubtedly be a fun and enjoyable week, a week like this can unfortunately also cause some inconvenience. As the board, we will do everything we can to prevent any inconvenience to the neighborhood during EL CID week. With extra services from ourselves and many other volunteers, we believe we can minimize any problems. Given the high number of participants this year, we anticipate some disruption from bicycles on Hogewoerd and Levendaal. We will ensure that people are present to ensure bicycles don't block the sidewalks and front doors of Hogewoerd and Levendaal.

During the week, SSR will have an outdoor terrace, just like every year. This terrace will be dismantled starting at 9:00 PM, so that everyone is off the streets by 10:00 PM. Lights will be attached to the facade of our building. EL CID participants are required to leave our building at 1:00 AM on Mondays and at 2:00 AM from Tuesdays through Thursdays; there will be a lot of people on the streets for a short time around those times. Although we will be at the door ourselves, you will understand that it is impossible for us to keep this large group quiet. However, we will, of course, do our utmost.

If you experience any inconvenience this week, please let us know. You can reach us day or night at 071-5120900. You can also email me at praeses@ssr-leiden.nl.

We expect a good week. We therefore hope you won't need the contact information above.

Yours sincerely,

On behalf of the 120th board of the SSR-Leiden,

Kirsten van Egdom

h.t. Praeses Association
