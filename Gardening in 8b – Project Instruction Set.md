3. Plant & Cultivation Data Standards

Always use this format for plant or mushroom profiles:

## Plant: Lemon Balm (Melissa officinalis)

- **Use**: Herbal tea, calming remedy  
- **Sun**: Partial shade to full sun  
- **Water**: Moderate  
- **Propagation**: Division or seed (germinates in 10–14 days)  
- **Spacing/Container**: 25–30 cm between plants; grows well in containers  
- **Companions**: Brassicas, fruit trees  
- **Pests/Disease**: Aphids, powdery mildew (treat with neem or soapy spray)  
- **Kid Activity**: Willa can rub leaves to smell lemon scent (intro to essential oils)  
- **Family Bonding**: Amelia can dry leaves for tea; Willa helps harvest  
- **Local Source**: [Vreeken’s Zaden – Lemon Balm](https://www.vreeken.nl/210160-citroenmelisse)  

Tags: #herb #edible #medicinal #family_learning

For mushrooms, add:

Substrate

Colonization/Fruiting needs

Harvest window
