> **You’re soaking everything at once in your tub.**  
> Your tub’s practical soak fill = **~120 L** (~25–30 cm water depth). If you fill more/less, see the table at the end.

---

## What you’ll use (on hand)
- **Kristalsoda** (washing soda, Na₂CO₃)
- **Subliem Oxy Booster – Color** (sodium percarbonate)
- **Citric acid** *(optional neutralizer — finishing step)*
- Rubber gloves, a plastic jug/bucket for dissolving, a stirring tool, laundry bags optional

---

## SAFETY
- Gloves + ventilation. Avoid splashes in eyes.
- **Never** mix or use during this process: Vim (chlorine scouring powder), vinegar, Spiritus, oven cleaner, drain products, or dishwasher powder.
- Keep pets/kids out; floor may get slick.

---

## VISUAL CHEAT SHEET (for quick estimates)
- **120 L water** ≈ tub filled to **~2/3 height** (about *two hand-widths* below the overflow).  
- **Water temp 50–60 °C:** too hot to keep your whole hand in for more than **1–2 sec**, but safe for a brief dip.  
- **1 cup = 16 Tbsp** (heaped spoons are OK).  
- **Powder looks fully dissolved** when **no grains collect** in a clear mug pulled from the bath.

---

## DOSES FOR **120 L** (copy this block)
**Step 1 — Degrease Pre-Soak (20–30 min @ 55–60 °C)**  
- **Kristalsoda:** **360 g = 24 Tbsp = 1½ cups**

**Step 2 — Main Oxygen Soak (60–90 min @ 50–60 °C)**  
- **Kristalsoda:** **270 g = 18 Tbsp = 1 cup + 2 Tbsp**  
- **Subliem Oxy (Color):** **540 g = 36 Tbsp = 2¼ cups**

**Step 2b — Optional Lighter Second Soak (30–45 min, only if water was still dirty/odorous)**  
- **Kristalsoda:** **90 g = 6 Tbsp = ⅜ cup**  
- **Subliem Oxy (Color):** **360 g = 24 Tbsp = 1½ cups**

**Optional Neutralizing Dip (2–3 min)**  
- **Citric acid:** **60 g = 12 tsp = 4 Tbsp = ¼ cup** dissolved in **120 L cool water**

> Rules if you change fill: **Pre-soak soda = 3 g/L** · **Main soda = 2.25 g/L** · **Main oxy = 4.5 g/L** · **Second-soak soda = 0.75–1 g/L** · **Second-soak oxy = 3 g/L** · **Citric = 0.5 g/L**.  
> (Spoon weights are estimates; precision isn’t critical.)

## STEP-BY-STEP

### 0) Prep (5–10 min)
- [ ] Vacuum fabric thoroughly; use a **damp rubber glove** or squeegee to pull embedded dog hair from the weave.
- [ ] Remove inserts/foam. Turn covers **inside-out**; **zip closed**.
- [ ] **Colorfast test:** Mix a coffee mug of main-soak water (soda + oxy), dab a hidden seam for **5 min** on a white tissue. If the tissue only shows a faint grey/blue hue and **no obvious dye**, proceed.

### 1) Degrease Pre-Soak (20–30 min)
1. Fill to **~120 L** with **55–60 °C** water (hot tap + kettle if needed).  
   *Visual:* water line ~**two hand-widths below overflow**.
2. Dissolve **360 g (1½ cups) Kristalsoda**. **Stir** until clear.
3. Submerge covers; **agitate every 5–10 min** (push down, swish ends).  
4. **Drain completely.** Optional: quick warm shower rinse or **short washer spin** to purge the greasy liquor.

### 2) Main Oxygen Soak (60–90 min)
1. Refill with **fresh 120 L** at **50–60 °C**.  
2. Dissolve **270 g (1 cup + 2 Tbsp) Kristalsoda** **+** **540 g (2¼ cups) Oxy**. Stir to fully dissolve.  
3. Add covers. **Agitate every 15–20 min**.  
4. **Assess at 60 min:**

   **Water appearance guide**
   - **Tea-brown / milky grey with scum** → heavy soil/oils → **continue to 90 min** and plan **Step 2b**.  
   - **Light grey/blue tint only** (no sheen) → mostly dye tint → **stop here** and go to rinsing.  
   - **Oily rainbow sheen** on top at any time → extend to **90 min**, then do **Step 2b**.

### 2b) Optional Lighter Second Soak (30–45 min) — *only if needed*
1. Drain and refill fresh, same temp.  
2. Dissolve **90 g (⅜ cup) Kristalsoda + 360 g (1½ cups) Oxy**.  
3. Soak **30–45 min**, agitating a few times.  
4. **Total wet time cap:** keep all soaks combined **≤ ~3 hours** to protect seams/labels.

### 3) Rinse + Optional Neutralize (10–20 min)
- Rinse **warm → cool** until the fabric no longer feels **slick/soapy** when you rub it between fingers.  
- *(Optional but recommended after heavy soda use or hard water):* Fill with **cool water** and dissolve **60 g (¼ cup) citric acid**. **Dip 2–3 min**, swish, then **final clear-water rinse**.  
  *What this does:* neutralizes leftover alkalinity, helps minerals release, and leaves a softer handfeel.

### 4) Machine Finish & Dry
- Short **rinse/spin** or a **gentle cycle** with **no/low detergent** just to flush residues.  
- **Air-dry** flat or over racks; reshape seams while damp.  
  *Visual:* seams should lay flat without puckers; avoid high heat. Sun + breeze are perfect, but don’t bake dark grey in midday sun.

### 5) Inserts (if any)
- **Foam:** do **not** soak. Sprinkle **baking soda**, dwell **30–60 min**, vacuum.  
- **Fiberfill inserts:** lukewarm hand-wash, thorough rinse, squeeze (don’t wring), dry with sun/airflow.

---

## SUCCESS CHECKS
- Fabric feels **de-waxy** (not slick) and smells neutral.  
- Rinse water runs clear with **no oily film**.  
- After drying, seams and zipper tapes lie **flat** (no distortion).

---

## TROUBLESHOOTING
- **Still smells after drying:** Mist the **inside** with very dilute oxy (½ Tbsp in 500 mL warm water), lightly dampen, air out in sun/breeze.  
- **Dull/soapy feel:** Do a **short neutralizing dip** again (citric 0.5 g/L), then a clear rinse and spin.  
- **Color lift on test:** Lower temps to **40–45 °C**, halve Kristalsoda amounts, and shorten soaks to **30–45 min**.

## NEVER MIX / STORAGE
- **Chlorine (Vim)** × **acids** (citric/vinegar) or **alcohol (Spiritus)** → dangerous gases/solvents.  
- Keep **oven** and **drain** products isolated; never combine anything in drains.  
- Store **acids, alkalis, and chlorine** on **separate shelves**.
